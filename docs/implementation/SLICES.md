### Selection and truthful promotion

- Work is chosen by **accepted dependency and active authorization**, not by automatically advancing the lowest numbered slice. Resume an incomplete authorized branch/PR first; do not adopt I17's historical PR #20 as the new architecture without reconciliation.
- Only one agent/slice at a time unless independent work is explicitly authorized. A safe parked blocker may not immobilize detached test profiles or genuinely independent work.
- A slice can be checkpointed with `D PASS; L BLOCKED`, but cannot be represented as fully accepted if required L/H/GIT evidence is absent. Applicable T/E negatives and recovery paths must be tested at the actual boundaries.
- S01 architecture proofs, early E-06 format spike and the E-08 restart proof happen **before** dependent implementation; do not defer them to the final release.
- This map contains no manually maintained status fields. Read actual state from remote PR/merge/evidence artifacts. Any plan change that alters accepted behavior or weakens a proof boundary requires targeted approval and a logged supersession, not an invisible checklist edit.
